About

I'm an Electrical and Computer Engineering student at the University of Toronto (BASc + PEY Co-op), focused on embedded firmware, digital hardware, and the systems that connect the two. Most of my work lives in C++ close to the metal — driving PWM peripherals, reverse-engineering RTL, or building the control loops that make a robot arm move where it's supposed to.
